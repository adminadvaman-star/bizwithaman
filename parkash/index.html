FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

Ask on WhatsApp Call us
What is the minimum order quantity?

Most cage models start at 1,000 to 5,000 pieces. Fencing starts at 1,000 kg for the standard mesh. For other products the minimum is agreed on the quote, so send your quantity and we will confirm.

Do the prices include GST and delivery?

No. Catalogue prices are indicative ex-works rates and exclude GST and freight. Your written quote shows the final price for your size, quantity and location.

Can you make custom sizes?

Yes. Share the cage dimensions, number of tiers and birds per box, or the fencing height and mesh size you need, and we will quote for it.

Do you supply outside India?

Yes. Parkash equipment has reached buyers in Saudi Arabia and we welcome export enquiries. Packing and commercial documents for your port are arranged on order.

How is the order delivered?

We dispatch by road across India from our factory in Safidon, Jind. Delivery time depends on quantity and is stated on the quote.

What payment modes do you accept?

Cash, cheque, demand draft and card. Terms for bulk and export orders are agreed on the quote.

Can I visit the factory?

Yes. The unit in Village Tito Kheri, Safidon is open every day from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Call ahead and we will show you the cages being made.
